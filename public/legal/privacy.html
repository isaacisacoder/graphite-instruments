Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Calculations stay in your browser

Graphite performs calculations in your browser, including its local calculation workers. The application does not send expressions, graph coordinates, imported workspaces or results to a calculation server. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or application tracking cookies.

Saved work and device storage

Local browser storage retains calculator history, Ans and variables, settings, specialist drafts and matching results, graph functions, points, parameters, favorites and recent instruments. Undo history is session-only. Browser caches and a service worker store application files so the instruments can work offline after caching. Sound and appearance preferences are stored locally; sound starts off for new users.

Imported files are parsed locally. Exports are downloaded to your device. Copy controls write selected results to your clipboard only when activated. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see saved work. Storage may be cleared by you or your browser; it is not a backup service.

Hosting and external links

The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives web requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visits made while signed out. Its handling of request information is covered by the GitHub privacy statement and Pages documentation. Application assets, including fonts, are served with this site; Graphite does not load remote fonts or third-party calculation APIs.

Following a GitHub link opens that service and is subject to its policies. Public issue reports are public: do not include private calculations, financial details or other sensitive information.

Your controls

You can export graph workspaces before clearing browser site data. Use your browser’s site-storage controls to remove saved work, caches and the service worker. No account deletion request is needed because Graphite has no account system. For questions or corrections, open a project issue without sensitive information.
