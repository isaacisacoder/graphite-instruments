Last updated November 14, 2026

Graphite is intended for education, exploration and general-purpose calculations. Accuracy is tested, but cannot be guaranteed for every input or application.

Interpret results carefully

Floating-point results can contain rounding error. Numerical integration and graph analysis return bounded estimates rather than proofs. Root and extremum searches do not promise to find every solution. Graphs are sampled representations and may omit narrow or rapidly varying features. Symbolic results can depend on domain assumptions. Read each instrument’s error messages and limitations.
